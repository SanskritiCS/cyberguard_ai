1. PRODUCT VISION

Cyber threats are evolving faster than human awareness.

Most cybersecurity tools simply tell users:

“This is dangerous.”

CyberGuard AI should go further.

It should:

DETECT the threat
↓
EXPLAIN why it is dangerous
↓
WARN the user clearly
↓
RECOMMEND what to do
↓
PROTECT the user

The product should make cybersecurity understandable to a normal person without requiring technical knowledge.

The most important UX principle is:

Never show a threat score without explaining WHY the score exists and WHAT the user should do next.
